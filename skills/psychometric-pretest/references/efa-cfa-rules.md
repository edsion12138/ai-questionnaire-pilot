# EFA/CFA rules

## EFA
Preferred default for latent questionnaire development:
- extraction: Principal Axis Factoring;
- rotation: Direct Oblimin or Promax;
- factor count: parallel analysis + theory + scree pattern.

PCA is primarily a data-reduction technique and uses total variance. Varimax imposes orthogonality. Use them only when their assumptions match the research goal.

## CFA
Compare plausible competing models rather than fitting only the preferred one. Strong fit of a complex model is less persuasive if a simpler model fits similarly and is theoretically adequate.

For ordinal human survey data, estimator choice should match the measurement level and distribution.
