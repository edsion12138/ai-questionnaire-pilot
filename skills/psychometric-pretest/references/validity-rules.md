# Reliability and validity rules

## Internal consistency
Alpha and omega answer related but not identical questions. Prefer reporting both when feasible.

## Convergent validity
CR and AVE are useful summaries but should not override content validity. Borderline AVE can be acceptable when reliability, loadings, model fit, and theoretical coverage are adequate.

## Discriminant validity
Use both Fornell-Larcker and HTMT when feasible. A construct pair with high HTMT should trigger examination of wording overlap and theoretical boundaries.

## Criterion/external validity
A useful criterion should be related but not redundant. Extremely high correlations can undermine the case that a new scale adds unique measurement value.
